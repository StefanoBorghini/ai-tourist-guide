PORTOVENERE TERRITORY PACK v1
=============================
STATUS: RESEARCH READY — NOT YET PRODUCTION VERIFIED.

PURPOSE
Create the first production-oriented Territory Pack for the AI Tourist Guide. The app engine must remain independent from territorial content.

CORE RULES
- Separate FACT / TRADITION / LEGEND / HYPOTHESIS / UNVERIFIED.
- Preliminary GPS is not production GPS.
- Every factual claim retains a source.
- Time-sensitive access/opening/transport data must be rechecked.
- Never invent historical details.
- Use only original or explicitly licensed images.

POI: Chiesa di San Lorenzo [san_lorenzo]
Categories: religion, history, architecture, art
Preliminary GPS: 44.0418, 9.8488
Geofence: 50 m (NOT VERIFIED)
Story status: FACTS_PLUS_TRADITION_SEPARATION_REQUIRED
- Romanesque church with three naves; local tourism source dates its consecration to 1130 by Pope Innocent II.
- Facade has alternating black and white bands.
- The church houses the venerated Madonna Bianca.
- The cedar-trunk story is local devotional tradition and must not be presented as independently proven history.

POI: Chiesa di San Pietro [san_pietro]
Categories: religion, history, architecture, landscape
Preliminary GPS: 44.0359, 9.8527
Geofence: 50 m (NOT VERIFIED)
Story status: FACTS_AND_HYPOTHESES_MUST_BE_SEPARATED
- Church on the rocky promontory and one of Porto Venere's defining landmarks.
- The Gothic Genoese phase is dated by local tourism material to 1256–1277.
- Earlier Christian and possible Roman antecedents must be narrated as hypotheses where not independently verified.
- The church suffered later military damage and was restored in 1929–1934.

CRITICAL TEST CASE: BYRON
The location Cala dell'Arpaia/Grotta Byron is factual. The association with Byron and especially the claim that he swam from Lerici to Porto Venere are presented in local tourism material as tradition/what is said to have happened. AI narration must explicitly distinguish geography from literary tradition.
